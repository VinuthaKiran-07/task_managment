# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: React + Vite, with a small component and hook architecture chosen for clean state flow and fast local development.

## Users

Individuals who need a lightweight place to capture and work through a personal task list.

## Product Purpose

Taskboard makes it easy to capture, prioritize, and complete everyday work without leaving the browser. Success means a user can understand their workload at a glance and take the next action quickly.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The app supports task creation with a title, description, priority, completion state, editing, deletion, title search, All/Active/Completed filters, summary counts, validation, empty states, and localStorage persistence. It is a client-side learning project focused on clear React state flow.

## Evidence on Hand

No external brand assets or product evidence were supplied. All sample tasks are illustrative and clearly replaceable.

## Product Principles

- Capture should feel immediate.
- Progress should be visible without noise.
- Every control should explain its consequence.
- State ownership should be easy to follow in code.
